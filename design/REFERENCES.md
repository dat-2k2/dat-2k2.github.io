# Mobbin references

Researched with Mobbin `search_sections` / `search_screens` (web). What each one contributed:

| # | Reference | Section | What I took |
|---|-----------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | [Freshman — hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/cc46fe95-d2fb-46a6-927a-96c9bc6d5ecc) | Hero | The name as the hero, set very large, with a short line of copy under it. No photo or illustration needed. |
| 2 | [GitHub ReadME Project — profile hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/10928b8e-852d-4424-b248-8028e8ff92d5) | Hero / About | Small uppercase mono "meta" label above the body copy, which suits an engineering profile. |
| 3 | [Figma portfolio template — work experience](https://mobbin.com/screens/928c9e80-3fc4-44cd-9d7c-561a3b0f26d3) | Experience | A fixed date column on the left and the role and company on the right, so dates scan in a single column. |
| 4 | [Wise — timeline](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/56d6864d-7fa5-4649-85cd-a2730ff775ce) | Experience | A thin vertical rail with dots. I made the current role's dot the only filled one, in the accent color. |
| 5 | [Lightdash — story timeline](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4a6efa26-8500-4346-a229-ba23004bce0f) | Experience | Hairline separators and quiet type rather than heavy cards, plus a single accent for markers. |
| 6 | [Wild — project cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/bd58336d-6de6-4678-aafd-10089a509897) | Projects | Small mono tech-stack chips under each project description. |
| 7 | [Lovable "Sarah Chen" portfolio — projects](https://mobbin.com/screens/d9550298-d9ea-4821-94d8-8e31f15f94e1) | Projects | Card anatomy: a colored eyebrow, then the title, description and tags, with a separate panel on the side. I used that panel for metrics (1.3×, 10×, <30s) instead of an image. |
| 8 | [Dribbble — profile about / skills](https://mobbin.com/screens/97e6a038-31e0-4818-86f9-61fb2a04fb0b) | Skills | Skills grouped under small labels as wrapped chip rows, rather than progress bars. |
| 9 | [Mother Design — footer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/23a43fc2-e616-4b49-945c-4a0dd97df268) | Contact / Footer | A large typographic contact (the email is the CTA) above a thin, ruled footer. |

What I deliberately avoided: gradient blobs and stock 3D renders (Framer), photo-heavy cards (TinyWins) and multicolor year blocks (Revolut). They work against a clean, single-accent engineering look.
